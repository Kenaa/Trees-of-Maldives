# Photographs

Put photo files here and reference them from `data/trees.js` as `photos/filename.jpg`.

- Name them after the record: `mle-0017.jpg`, `mle-0017-b.jpg`.
- Resize to about 1600px on the long edge before adding them. Full-size phone photos
  make the site slow on exactly the mobile connections most readers will be using.
- Every photo needs `alt` text in the record. Describe what is in the frame.
- Avoid photographs in which people are recognisable, unless they have agreed.
